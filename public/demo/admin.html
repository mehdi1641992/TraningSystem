Winter Academy 14 — Academy Vision

Every child in Bangladesh experiences an excellent education. We build a cohort of outstanding teachers who lead change from inside the classroom and beyond it.

Key Training Goals

1. Build instructional excellence through daily practice and feedback.
2. Develop a shared language of leadership, equity and inclusion.
3. Embed reflective practice through journaling and coaching.
4. Prepare Participants for placement in high-need classrooms.

Structural Pillars

Leadership Equity & Inclusion Community & Culture Learning & Growth
Pillars are a separate, non-colour-coded tag that can be attached to any session on top of its session type.

Statement of Purpose

We exist so that the children who need the most get the most. Academy is the beginning of a lifetime of leadership, not the end of a training week.
